The research journal

Two checks worth slowing down for.

Guide 01 / Size and fit

How to use a SuperBuy spreadsheet without guessing your size

Begin with something you already own that fits well. Lay it flat and record the relevant measurements in centimeters. Compare those numbers with the specific seller's chart rather than relying on a familiar letter size.

  • Footwear: compare foot length with the model-specific chart. Foot length and insole length are different measurements.
  • Tops: compare chest width, shoulder width and body length. Check whether the chart uses flat width or circumference.
  • Trousers: check waist, rise and inseam. An elastic waist range is more informative than a single size label.

Guide 02 / Photo inspection

What QC photos can—and cannot—tell you

QC means quality control. When available, inspection photos can help you check the received item against the selected listing option. Look for mismatched colors, missing pieces, visible stains, uneven seams and inconsistent measurements.

Request a measurement photo when fit is uncertain. Photographs alone cannot establish authenticity, long-term durability or the exact feel of a fabric. Check the current return terms before placing an order.

For authenticity-sensitive purchases: choose an authorized retailer or an independently authenticated resale service.

Guide 03 / Budget

Build a total-cost comparison

A useful SuperBuy spreadsheet price comparison separates the item price from domestic delivery, purchasing-service fees, international freight, currency conversion and potential import charges.

Record the currency and the date checked. Keep unconfirmed charges marked as estimates. A low item price does not establish a low delivered cost.

Guide 04 / Destination

Prepare a parcel for your destination

For SuperBuy shipping preparation, compare packed weight and dimensions with the current terms of the available routes. Ask whether a quote uses actual weight or dimensional weight.

For US, UK and EU destinations, check the destination customs authority's current import requirements. Confirm product restrictions, tracking coverage, insurance terms and return options before dispatch.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is a product-research list used to organize source links and details such as category, size options and listing notes. Its contents depend on the publisher; a spreadsheet entry is not a guarantee of quality or authenticity.

Where do the links on this page take me?

The category buttons open the corresponding HipobuyFind category pages. All other navigation links open the HipobuyFind homepage. This page does not send orders to SuperBuy.

Is this an official SuperBuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent research guide with no official affiliation with SuperBuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Hermès or Dior. Brand names identify research subjects and do not imply endorsement.

Do spreadsheet prices include international shipping?

Do not assume they do. Check the listing and purchasing service separately for domestic delivery, service fees, international freight, currency conversion and potential import charges. This guide provides no live price or shipping quote.

Can QC photos prove an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help reveal visible condition, selected options and measurements, but they do not establish authenticity. For authenticity-sensitive purchases, use an authorized retailer or an independently authenticated resale service.

How should I choose a size?

Compare the seller's measurements with a garment that fits you, using the same units and measurement method. For shoes, check the model-specific chart and whether its measurements refer to foot length or insole length.
